<summary><strong>What changed in this PR</strong></summary>

This PR addresses the “ablegen als…” filename-conflict behavior in #2460, adding replacement or renaming choices when a converted document already exists in the case or its recycle bin.

**Changes:**
- Adds conflict prompts and updates the document list after replacement.
- Introduces staged replacement with tag restoration, recovery attempts, and partial-failure reporting.
- Adds 17 tests covering replacement, stale server state, filename limits, tagging, and failure handling.
