[copilot-pull-request-reviewer] This excludes widgets, but not the fields that own them. In a hierarchical AcroForm with separate fields on retained and excluded pages, a retained widget can reach its field's `/Parent`, then that parent's `/Kids`, including the excluded page's field. Removing that field's widget leaves its `/V` and `/DV` reachable and serialized, so confidential form values from excluded pages still appear in the part despite the correct page count. Please prune field branches with no retained widgets, or flatten retained widgets and detach the form hierarchy. Add a regression with distinct sibling-field values and check dictionary strings as well as streams.
