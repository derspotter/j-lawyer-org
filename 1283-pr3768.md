Addresses #1283 by allowing “Adressen - neu” to reset the displayed form after a contact was saved through the labels tab, while prompting before discarding subsequent edits.

- Adds opt-in reset behavior when reselecting an editor, including save, discard, cancel, and save-failure handling.
- Updates address state handling to preserve dropdown selections and dirty-state detection.
- Adds eight regression tests for editor reselection.
